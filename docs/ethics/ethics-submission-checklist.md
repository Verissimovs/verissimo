## Key Claims To Make Accurately

- The project is a prototype, not an official service.
- The study evaluates usability and explainability.
- Participants use fictional scenarios only.
- No real special-category data is required.
- The researcher will not provide benefits, legal, financial or immigration advice.
- Participation is voluntary and participants can stop or skip tasks.
- Findings will be anonymised.

## Do Not Claim Yet

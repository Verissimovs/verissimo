# Recruitment And Screening Notes

## Recruitment Aim

Recruit 5 to 8 adult participants for scenario-based usability testing of the VERISSIMO prototype.

## Inclusion Criteria

- Aged 18 or over.
- Able to use a web browser.
- Able to provide informed consent.
- Comfortable using fictional benefits-related scenarios.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Under 18.
- Unable to provide informed consent.
- Currently seeking urgent benefits, legal, financial or immigration advice from the study.
- Expects VERISSIMO or the researcher to assess their real entitlement.
- Uncomfortable with fictional questions about disability, care needs, income or end-of-life rules.

## Recruitment Message Draft

I am looking for adult participants to help test the usability of VERISSIMO, a web prototype for a computing dissertation. The prototype explains how selected UK benefits eligibility rules apply to fictional scenarios. This is not official benefits advice and participants should not enter real personal information. The session takes about 30 to 45 minutes and involves completing tasks and giving feedback about clarity and ease of use.

## Screening Questions

1. Are you aged 18 or over?
2. Are you comfortable using a web browser?
3. Do you understand this is a usability test of a prototype, not benefits advice?
4. Are you willing to use fictional scenario data only?
5. Would fictional benefits-related questions about disability, care needs, income or end-of-life rules cause you distress?

If the answer to question 5 is yes, do not include the participant.
